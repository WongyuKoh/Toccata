## Final architecture to detail = "W1+" (the judge's recommended_design_ko, verbatim in concepts_result.json → judge.recommended_design_ko)
Short version: each key is an individual PETG seesaw on a Ø4 SUS304 rod lying in a balance rail (pivot ≈ y141, z21.5). Its hidden tail carries a steel capstan (M3 button-head screw in a nut trap, white y≈188.3, black y≈180.6). The capstan lifts a weighted lever: printed carrier (common to white and black) + SS400 steel block 9×50×21.5 (≈76 g, mechanically captured — snap lip or Ø2 cross pin, epoxy only against rattle) hung on a Ø3 SUS304 rod through 4 printed fins (lever pivot ≈ y203.5, z33.5). Gravity on the lever returns the key; no spring in the base design. Rest = tail felt 1.5T on the rear shelf (z≈20). Bottom = v3 front felt 3T + steel front-top corner (rounded R≥2 + printed/felt cap) meeting a steel up-stop rail 3T×19 with 2T felt, fastened with M3 thumb screws into ≥3 mm pin bosses with nut traps (4 per module); black-key lever bays get a 1.8 mm pad so black levers stop at their own height. The v3 front hook is kept only as a non-touching keeper 0.5 mm above the crossbar (anti-lift / transport). The key's open notch on the rod is R2.55 lined with 0.5T bushing cloth. Optional assist-spring seat (Ø2 steel guide pin + pocket, reaction into the bolted rail) drawn but empty (spring d0.35 / OD6 / ~11 active turns, L0/D ≤ 4, C ≤ 16 — only if stage-0 friction pushes t40 over 37.6 ms). Sensor bar, stripboard, ribbon lane, control board (y145.5–195.5) and USB exit stay where v3 has them; magnet moves to y67. Frame 164.5 × 212, total instrument 1254 × 410 unchanged; dust cover rises from z59 to ≈z73.
W1-B (board moved under key front, pivot y160, front/back ratio ≈2.1) is NOT the base — only document it as an option.

## Every item in judge.must_fix_before_detail and judge.grafts must be resolved or explicitly answered in the final model
(They include: up-stop rail hold-down and load check with a 6-key chord ≈360 N; black-bay 1.8 mm pads and re-simulation; 4-DOF free-notch lift model with keeper and ≤0.2 mm lift target, 90° tilt retention; ghost re-trigger check after ff/fff with a 2 N held finger (key-front lift < ~2 mm); print plan with supports and capstan as a steel screw → ~5.56 kg / ~371 h; repetition margin with doubled friction and the optional spring; Flores (or measured-type) felt contact with effective e ≈ 0.35; all clearances ≥ 1.0 mm after ±0.3 FDM including x/y/z sweeps with neighbours in rest/dip/ff poses; balance-rail saddles that locate the rod in y and clear the key ≥1 mm; capstan-felt static set vs up-stop timing and the adjustment procedure; lever hub spacing (0.3 mm gap or washer), fins in E|F and B|C gaps, F#/G# beam jog and roll moment; ribs at x76.5 and x91.5 under the rear shelf span over the USB plug; corrected key-removal procedure; cost with real small-quantity steel prices, shipping and cutting labour; cover/rear-bar relationship; module mass ≈1.5 kg vs R18; cover resting on pins/rail now that the spine is gone.)
Also cover what W1 never modelled: the end parts (left A0/A#0/B0, 47 mm wide, and right C8, 39.5 mm) with the same mechanism; module dovetails vs the new tails/levers (S1's verifier found the v3 rear dovetails collide with tails); what goes in the R29 spare-key storage bay (inner 338.8 × 172 × 77) now that keys are individual (e.g. spare white keys C-type/D-type, black key, levers, steel, rods).

## Conventions
mm, g, N, ms. z = 0 desk, y = 0 white key front lip, +y away from the player, x across the octave (x = 0 at the C left nominal boundary, module width 164.5). White key heads: v3 KC…KB x ranges; black keys: v3 KC#…KA# x ranges (see v3_extract P-table). Assume printer bed 220×220, 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 layers, Bambu PETG Basic (density 1.25, E_flex 1.95 GPa, tensile 51 MPa, HDT 71 °C), 15 g/h print rate.
Korean for all user-facing text (design.md, labels on drawings). Numbers in drawings must come from geometry.json (no hand-typed dimensions).
